№ 09

Questions, Answered

Is IV therapy safe?

Yes, when administered by a qualified nurse after a health screening. We follow strict hygiene protocols and medical oversight.

How long does it take?

Drips typically take 30 minutes to an hour. Injections are faster — around five to ten minutes.

Will I feel results straight away?

Some clients report feeling refreshed or more energised shortly after, but experiences vary and no effects are guaranteed.

Can everyone have this?

Not always. Certain medical conditions, allergies or medications may make IV therapy unsuitable — which is why we require a consultation.

How often can I have treatment?

Frequency is determined by clinical assessment following a thorough consultation with one of our healthcare professionals.

Are there any side effects?

IV therapy is generally very safe, but there are risks you should be aware of before treatment. These are discussed fully during your consultation.
